The four levels follow the ambition levels of the UK 2050 Calculator and EUCalc (DECC 2010; Kockat et al. 2019). The Reference holds current practice; business-as-usual technology change applies in every run. Ambitious is a documented frontier: a value achieved in New Zealand or elsewhere, an official projection, a peer-reviewed scenario or a documented requirement, and is cited; for levers with NZ case studies it is the top-15% value of the case distribution (Harrell–Davis quantile; the top-15% convention of the EU Taxonomy). Moderate is the midpoint of the Reference and Ambitious. The Theoretical Limit is the logical bound where one exists, otherwise the best value observed; it is a thought experiment, not a target. Levels phase in from 2026 to 2035; L9 follows its own path to 2050, and TL1 is limited by the fastest observed change. Avoid acts on floor area; Switch and Improve act on carbon per m² (PAS 2080:2023, Clause 4.3). Values are in Supplementary Table 5.
