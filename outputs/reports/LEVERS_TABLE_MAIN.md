The four levels follow the ambition levels of the UK 2050 Calculator and EUCalc (DECC 2010; Kockat et al. 2019). The Reference holds current practice; business-as-usual technology change applies in every run. Ambitious is a documented frontier: a value achieved in New Zealand or elsewhere, an official projection, a peer-reviewed scenario or a documented requirement, and is cited; for levers with NZ case studies it is the top-15% value of the case distribution (Harrell–Davis quantile; the top-15% convention of the EU Taxonomy). Moderate is the midpoint of the Reference and Ambitious. Theoretical Limit = the best value observed, internationally where a comparable measure exists, otherwise in New Zealand, or a documented scenario bound; a logical bound only where physically attainable by choice or design. It is a thought experiment, not a target. Levels phase in from 2026 to 2035; L10 follows its own path to 2050, and L1 at TL is limited by the fastest observed change. L1–L5 (Avoid) reduce floor area; L6–L8 (Switch) and L9–L10 (Improve) reduce carbon per m² (PAS 2080:2023, Clause 4.3). Values and sources in Extended Data Table 1.
